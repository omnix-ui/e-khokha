Frequently Asked Questions

Open My Orders from your Account section to view your orders, payment status and delivery information.
You can manage your saved addresses from the Saved Addresses section. For an already placed order, address changes may not be possible after processing has started.
Check the coupon's minimum order value, validity period and usage limits. Only active and eligible coupons can be applied.
After a successful checkout, you will see the order success screen. Your order will also appear in My Orders.
You can review a product after purchasing and receiving it. Reviews are checked before becoming publicly visible.
